\# Key Business Insights

\## Reliance Jio – 10-Year Business Intelligence \& Growth Analysis



\### 1. Strong Long-Term Revenue Growth



Reliance Jio's revenue increased from ₹20,154 crore in FY2017–18 to ₹1,28,871 crore in FY2025–26.



\- Revenue CAGR displayed in the dashboard: 26.10%.

\- FY2018–19 recorded the highest revenue YoY growth of 101.76%.

\- Revenue continued to increase across the financial years shown.



\### 2. Significant Improvement in Profitability



Profit After Tax (PAT) increased from ₹723 crore in FY2017–18 to ₹28,173 crore in FY2025–26.



\- PAT CAGR displayed in the dashboard: 58.07%.

\- PAT margin increased from 3.59% to 21.86%.

\- This represents an improvement of 18.27 percentage points.



The improvement in PAT margin indicates stronger profit conversion relative to revenue.



\### 3. Revenue and PAT Performance in FY2025–26



The latest financial year displayed in the dashboard reports:



| Metric | FY2025–26 |

|---|---:|

| Revenue | ₹1,28,871 crore |

| PAT | ₹28,173 crore |

| Revenue YoY Growth | 12.91% |

| PAT YoY Growth | 13.62% |

| PAT Margin | 21.86% |



PAT growth exceeded revenue growth, indicating an improvement in profitability during the latest reported year.



\### 4. Changing Growth Patterns



FY2018–19 experienced exceptionally high revenue growth of 101.76%, reflecting an early expansion phase.



In subsequent years, revenue growth moderated. By FY2025–26, revenue growth stood at 12.91%.



This highlights the difference between early-stage expansion and the more moderate growth observed in later years.



\### 5. Growth vs Profitability Analysis



The Growth vs Profitability Matrix compares revenue growth and PAT margin across financial years.



The dashboard displays historical median reference levels of:



\- Revenue YoY Growth: 15.98%.

\- PAT Margin: 19.65%.



In FY2025–26, revenue growth of 12.91% was below the historical median, while PAT margin of 21.86% remained above its historical median.



This suggests that profitability remained strong despite slower revenue growth.



\### 6. Overall Business Observations



The analysis highlights three major patterns:



1\. Sustained revenue expansion over the financial years analyzed.

2\. Substantial improvement in PAT and PAT margin.

3\. A shift toward comparatively moderate revenue growth alongside stronger profitability.



\### Conclusion



The analysis demonstrates how Reliance Jio's financial performance evolved from FY2017–18 to FY2025–26.



By combining PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI and DAX, this project translates historical financial information into measurable business indicators and interactive visual analysis.
